# Abstract writing

Draft the abstract after the article's evidence is stable. Include only details supported by the brief, methods, results, and verified source cards.

1. State the problem or objective and the gap the article addresses.
2. Name the approach or evidence base at the level needed to understand the work.
3. Report the principal result without adding numbers absent from the source material.
4. Give a restrained conclusion that follows from that result.

Respect the requested word range and any required structured headings. Do not add citations unless the task explicitly requires them. Do not use claims, methods, results, or implications that are missing from the article evidence.
