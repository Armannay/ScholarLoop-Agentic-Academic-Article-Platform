# Literature matrix

Build comparisons from supplied or verified SourceCards. Keep each row traceable to a source id.

For each source, record only available facts:

- research question or topic;
- method, population, corpus, or data;
- reported finding and the evidence supporting it;
- stated limitations or unresolved uncertainty;
- relevance to the article's question or gap.

Group sources by themes, methods, or disagreements rather than listing them one at a time. Mark missing fields as unknown. Do not infer a result from a title or snippet, and do not fabricate authors, dates, venues, or quotations.
