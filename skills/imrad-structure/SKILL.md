# IMRaD structure

Use Introduction, Methods, Results, and Discussion as a reasoning sequence, not merely headings.

- **Introduction:** establish the problem and gap, then state the objective or contribution.
- **Methods:** describe design, materials or data, procedures, and analysis with enough detail to assess reproducibility.
- **Results:** report findings in the order of the questions or analyses; separate observations from interpretation.
- **Discussion:** interpret results against the question and evidence, then state limitations and implications.

Keep each claim in the section where it is supported. Adapt the sequence for the article type; never invent methods, results, or data to fill a section.
